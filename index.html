The Fuselage

Whiteboard Instructions

    Size
    FuselageDraw a long, slightly tapered horizontal tube — wider at the front, narrower toward the tail.
    WingsStart at the lower-mid fuselage. Sweep back and taper to a point. Mirror on both sides.
    Horizontal StabSmaller wings at the tail. Same sweep angle as the main wing, just smaller.
    Vertical FinTriangle above the tail — taller than wide, sweeping forward at the top.
    CockpitA curved bump near the front-top of the fuselage with a few window lines.
    EngineRound circle on the wing (or nose for a prop plane). Add a few thrust-line chevrons.
